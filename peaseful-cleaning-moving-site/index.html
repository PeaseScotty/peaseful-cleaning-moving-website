How It Works

A straightforward process.

Submitting a request is the first step and does not guarantee scheduling or acceptance of the job.

  1. 1

    Tell Us What You Need

    Call, text, email, or complete the service-request form with basic information about the cleaning, moving, or hauling project.

  2. 2

    Discuss the Details

    Review the timing, general location, amount of work, stairs, major items, accessibility preferences, transportation needs, and other important details.

  3. 3

    Review the Expected Scope

    Peaseful will discuss anticipated work, availability, scheduling, transportation arrangements, and expected cost before service is confirmed whenever possible.

  4. 4

    Confirm the Service

    Agree on the date, timing, responsibilities, cost, and next steps.

Our Approach

Service built around you.

Every cleaning project, move, and household transition is different. Peaseful Cleaning N Moving begins by listening to what matters to each customer and developing a practical plan that respects their choices, belongings, schedule, comfort, communication preferences, accessibility needs, and budget.

We listen first

Customers can explain their goals, concerns, priorities, accessibility needs, and preferred way of communicating.

We respect your choices

Customers remain involved in decisions about their belongings, schedule, service priorities, and the work being completed.

We adapt when possible

The service approach may be adjusted based on mobility, disability, age, communication preferences, sensory needs, household circumstances, or the complexity of the transition.

We communicate clearly

Peaseful aims to explain the anticipated work, timing, responsibilities, transportation arrangements, and expected cost before service begins whenever possible.

Peaseful's person-centered approach means services are planned with the customer, not simply for the customer.

Accessibility planning

Respectful support for different needs.

Peaseful Cleaning N Moving welcomes customers with disabilities and customers who may benefit from additional time, accessibility planning, mobility considerations, sensory accommodations, or a preferred communication method.

Customers are encouraged to share reasonable accessibility or communication preferences when requesting service. Requested adjustments can be discussed, but availability depends on the specific project and circumstances.

Cost and moving assistance

Cost should not prevent you from asking for help.

Moving is often necessary during major life transitions, and cost can become a serious barrier. Peaseful Cleaning N Moving believes everyone should be able to ask what options may be available.

Limited reduced-cost services or referrals to community resources may be available in some circumstances. Availability is not guaranteed and may depend on funding, eligibility, scheduling, the requested service, and available community resources.

We will listen respectfully, explain any known options, and help customers understand the next step whenever possible.

FAQ

Questions customers often ask.

What services does Peaseful provide?

Services may include cleaning, moving, packing, loading, transportation, unloading, basic setup, junk removal, and hauling assistance.

How do I request service?

Customers may complete the service-request form, call or text (406) 258-8558, or email peasefullcleaningmoving@gmail.com.

Does submitting the form confirm an appointment?

No. Submitting a request allows Peaseful to review the project and follow up about details, pricing, scheduling, and availability.

Do you provide the moving truck?

Transportation arrangements depend on the specific project. Describe your transportation needs when requesting service so the details can be confirmed.

Do you offer small moves?

Customers are encouraged to ask about small moves, storage moves, single-item moves, and other projects. Availability depends on scheduling and the work requested.

Can you work with customers with disabilities?

Peaseful welcomes customers who may benefit from additional time, accessibility planning, mobility considerations, sensory accommodations, or a preferred communication method. Customers are encouraged to share reasonable preferences when requesting service.

What items can you haul?

Accepted items depend on safety, capacity, disposal rules, and local regulations. Customers should describe the items before scheduling.

Is reduced-cost assistance guaranteed?

No. Reduced-cost services, referrals, discounts, and community funding are limited and are not guaranteed.

How can I contact Peaseful?

Call or text (406) 258-8558, email peasefullcleaningmoving@gmail.com, visit peasefulcleaningmoving.com, or complete the service-request form.
