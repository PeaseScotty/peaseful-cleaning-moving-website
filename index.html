How It Works

A straightforward process.

Each project begins with conversation, planning, and a clear understanding of the work ahead.

  1. Tell Us What You Need

    Call, text, or email with information about the cleaning, move, or hauling project.

  2. Discuss the Details

    Review the location, timing, amount of work, special items, stairs, accessibility needs, and any other relevant information.

  3. Receive an Estimate

    Peaseful will discuss the expected scope and cost before the work is scheduled.

  4. Confirm the Service

    Agree on the date, timing, and final details.

About

Local help with a personal touch.

Moving, cleaning, downsizing, and clearing a home can be stressful. Scotty Pease created Peaseful Cleaning N Moving to make these transitions more manageable through communication, respect, and practical help.

Peaseful Cleaning N Moving offers practical support for daily life changes that may feel overwhelming. The goal is to help customers move forward with clarity and calm.

Every project is approached with care, attention to detail, and a willingness to listen carefully before work begins.

Founded by Scotty Pease

Peaseful Cleaning N Moving was created to offer practical help during cleaning projects, moves, home transitions, and moments when an extra set of hands can make the process feel more manageable.

The business emphasizes respectful communication, careful handling of belongings, clear expectations, and support that adapts to the customer's needs.

FAQ

Questions customers often ask.

What services do you provide?

Services may include cleaning, moving, packing, loading, transportation, unloading, setup, junk removal, and hauling assistance.

How do I request an estimate?

Customers can call, text, or email to discuss the project and request an estimate.

Do you provide the moving truck?

Truck and transportation arrangements depend on the specific job. Please confirm vehicle needs when requesting an estimate.

Do you offer small moves?

Customers should contact the business to discuss the size and scope of the project.

Can you help seniors or people with disabilities?

Peaseful welcomes customers who may need additional patience, accessibility planning, or assistance communicating the details of a move or cleaning project.

What items can you haul away?

Accepted items depend on safety, vehicle capacity, disposal requirements, and local regulations. Customers should describe the items before scheduling.

Can you help me find assistance with the cost?

Peaseful may be able to connect qualifying customers with a funding source. Eligibility, approval, and available funding are determined by the funding program and are not guaranteed.

How do you reduce waste?

Whenever practical and with the customer's approval, Peaseful reuses or repurposes materials and looks for appropriate opportunities to donate or recycle items.

What areas do you serve?

Peaseful serves Missoula and surrounding communities. Call or text to confirm availability for your location.

What are your business hours?

Call or text to discuss current availability.
