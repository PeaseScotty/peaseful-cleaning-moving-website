Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains the information Peaseful Cleaning & Moving may receive when customers contact the business or submit a service inquiry.

Business information

Peaseful Cleaning & Moving is operated by Peaseful Cleaning LLC.

Contact Peaseful Cleaning & Moving by calling or texting (406) 258-8558, emailing connect@peacefulcleaningmoving.com, or visiting https://peasefulcleaningmoving.com.

Information customers may choose to provide

Customers may voluntarily provide information while requesting cleaning, moving, hauling, or household support. This may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • General location
  • Service details
  • Preferred date
  • Accessibility or communication preferences
  • Information about moving or cleaning needs

Google Form inquiries

The website links to a Google Form used to collect service inquiries. Information submitted through Google Forms is also subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

Submitting the form does not confirm an appointment, price, reduced-cost assistance, or acceptance of the job.

Sensitive information

Customers should not submit highly sensitive personal information through ordinary email, text, or the initial service-request form. Please do not send Social Security numbers, banking information, benefit numbers, medical records, passwords, entry codes, or detailed diagnoses.

How information is used

Information provided through direct contact or the service-request form may be used to respond to questions, discuss services, plan scheduling, understand access needs, follow up about availability, and maintain reasonable business records.

Privacy questions may be sent to connect@peacefulcleaningmoving.com or discussed by calling or texting (406) 258-8558.
