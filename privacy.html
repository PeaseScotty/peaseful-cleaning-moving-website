Privacy notice

Peaseful Cleaning N Moving may receive information when a person contacts the business by phone, text, email, or inquiry. This may include a name, phone number, email address, general service location, cleaning or moving details, and photographs that are later voluntarily provided through an agreed method.

Customers should not send highly sensitive information by ordinary email or text. Please use a secure and appropriate method if private details are necessary.

Information provided through direct contact is used to respond to questions, discuss services, schedule work, and maintain reasonable business records. Contact connect@peasefulcleaningmoving.com or (406) 258-8558 with privacy questions.
